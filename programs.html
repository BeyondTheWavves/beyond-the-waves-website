Programs

Lessons for every swimmer

Children & Beginners

Private, semi-private, and group lessons that build water safety, comfort, and foundational skills from the very first class — shaped around your child's pace, not a fixed curriculum.

Adults & Seniors

Judgment-free instruction for adult beginners, returning swimmers, and seniors — learn-to-swim, stroke refinement, and safe, independent movement in the water.

Stroke Development

Focused technique correction — freestyle, backstroke, and beyond — for swimmers looking to swim faster and more efficiently.

Adaptive Swim & Special Needs

One-on-one lessons with adapted teaching methods and a patience-first approach, for swimmers with disabilities or specific physical or sensory needs.
