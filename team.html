Our Founders

Why Beyond the Waves Was Created

Jannah, our Founder & Head Instructor, is a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) candidate, and Demi, our Co-Founder, is also a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) candidate. Their education in human movement, motor learning, and individualized progression shapes how every instructor at Beyond the Waves is trained to teach.

That perspective — understanding how bodies move, adapt, and learn — is why Beyond the Waves was created: to bring a more thoughtful, individualized approach to swim education.

Consistency

Every Instructor. One Standard.

Not every Beyond the Waves instructor needs to be a physical therapist. But every instructor is trained to teach through the same movement-informed philosophy.

Through The Beyond Standard, our instructors learn to consider the swimmer's movement, comfort, ability, goals, communication, progression, and confidence — not simply whether a skill was completed.

This allows the Beyond experience to remain consistent as our team grows.
