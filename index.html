Our Philosophy

The Beyond Standard

01 — SAFETY

Safety

Every decision, every lesson, every pool partnership starts with safety. It's non-negotiable, not a marketing line — real breath control, real judgment, and real comfort in the water come first, always.

02 — PERSONALIZED

Personalized

No two swimmers learn the same way. Every lesson is shaped around the person in front of us — their pace, comfort level, and goals — instead of a fixed curriculum. Lessons are available private, semi-private, or in small groups.

03 — CONFIDENCE

Confidence

We measure success by how safe and capable a swimmer feels, not just their stroke technique. Fear reduction and trust-building matter as much as skill — and every milestone, no matter how small, gets celebrated.

"Every swimmer should leave feeling safer, stronger, and more confident than when they started."
About Us

Beyond the Waves Aquatics is a swim education and aquatic wellness company based in New York and Long Island, dedicated to helping people of all ages develop water safety, confidence, independence, and lifelong swimming skills. We offer private, semi-private, and group instruction for children, adults, seniors, athletes, and beginners — every lesson personalized to the swimmer's pace, goals, and comfort level.

THE PROBLEM

Our Problem

Many children and adults don't have access to safe, personalized, high-quality swim instruction. Some are afraid of the water, have had negative past experiences, require additional support, or simply get lost in traditional group lessons.

THE SOLUTION

Our Solution

We provide flexible, professional, and personalized swim programs — led by instructors with 3+ years of experience — that help swimmers become safer in and around the water, build confidence, learn proper technique, and develop lifelong aquatic skills.

Programs

Lessons for every swimmer

Children & Beginners

Private, semi-private, and group lessons that build water safety, comfort, and foundational skills from the very first class — shaped around your child's pace, not a fixed curriculum.

Adults & Seniors

Judgment-free instruction for adult beginners, returning swimmers, and seniors — learn-to-swim, stroke refinement, and safe, independent movement in the water.

Stroke Development

Focused technique correction — freestyle, backstroke, and beyond — for swimmers looking to swim faster and more efficiently.

Adaptive Swim & Special Needs

One-on-one lessons with adapted teaching methods and a patience-first approach, for swimmers with disabilities or specific physical or sensory needs.

FAQs

Good to know

What should my swimmer bring to their first lesson?

A swimsuit, towel, and goggles. We'll send full arrival instructions — plus your required waiver and medical form — in your welcome email right after booking.

What's your cancellation policy?

Reschedule for free with 24+ hours notice; cancellations under 24 hours forfeit the session. If we ever need to cancel — due to illness, an emergency, or unsafe weather — you'll always get a free make-up, never charged as a missed session.

Do you offer make-up lessons?

Yes — one make-up lesson is allowed per month for cancellations made with proper notice, valid for 60 days.

What ages and abilities do you teach?

All ages and abilities — children, adults, seniors, competitive athletes, individuals with disabilities, and adult beginners easing into the water for the first time.

What are your payment and refund policies?

We accept credit/debit card, Zelle, Venmo, and cash. Single sessions are paid at booking, packages are paid upfront, and memberships bill automatically on the 25th of each month. Packages are fully refundable within 30 days of purchase (used sessions prorated at the single-session rate); memberships can be cancelled anytime with 30 days notice.

How will I know how my swimmer is progressing?

You'll get a progress update every 4–6 lessons, tied to specific milestones in our leveling system — so you always know what your swimmer is working on and what's next.

Do you travel to our pool or building?

Yes — for lessons at your building or a private pool, a $20–30 travel fee applies depending on location.
