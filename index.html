Our Philosophy

The Beyond Standard

01 — SAFETY

Safety

Every great swimmer starts the same way — feeling safe enough to try. We build that foundation first: real breath control, real judgment, real comfort in the water. No shortcuts, no rushing — just the fundamentals that let confidence grow naturally, lesson by lesson.

02 — INDIVIDUALITY

Individuality

Your pace is the right pace. Some swimmers pick up strokes in a week, some need a whole summer just to put their face in the water — and both are exactly on track. Lessons are private, semi-private, or small group, shaped around your swimmer instead of a fixed curriculum.

03 — COMMUNITY

Community

Here, we're not a school — we're a family. We support each other through every fear, every setback, and every breakthrough, in and out of the water. Parents cheering from the pool deck, instructors who remember your kid's story, other families who've been exactly where you are — that's what makes the difference.

"Every swimmer deserves to feel strong, safe, and capable in the water."
About Us

Beyond the Waves Aquatics is a New York-based swim school built around one idea: swim instruction should meet the swimmer where they are. Whether that's a toddler taking a first dip, an adult swimming for the first time, or an athlete chasing a faster split — every lesson is personal, structured, and safety-first.

As we grow, Beyond the Waves is building toward aquatic and physical therapy programming, additional NYC locations, and eventually franchising — always guided by The Beyond Standard.

Programs

Lessons for every swimmer

LANE 01

Children & Beginners

LANE 02

Adults & Seniors

Judgment-free instruction for adult beginners, returning swimmers, and seniors focused on safety, fitness, and independence in the water.

LANE 03

Athletes & Stroke Development

Stroke refinement and conditioning work for competitive and recreational athletes looking to swim faster and more efficiently.

LANE 04

Adaptive Swim & Special Needs

One-on-one lessons designed around individual needs, for swimmers with disabilities or specific physical or sensory considerations.

FAQs

Good to know

What should my swimmer bring to their first lesson?

A swimsuit, towel, and goggles. We'll send full arrival instructions in your welcome email after booking.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free reschedule with 24+ hours notice. Under 24 hours forfeits the session. Weather cancellations always get a free make-up.

Do you offer make-up lessons?

Yes — one make-up lesson is allowed per month for cancellations made with proper notice, valid for 60 days.

What ages do you teach?

All ages — from toddlers to seniors, beginners to competitive athletes.
